# SOMEWHAT INTELLIGENT surreal shirt concepts

This set moves the shirt into the newer red-car/data-center image world. Its
rule is documentary surrealism: photograph an ordinary industrial scene
plainly, then introduce one impossible spatial or physical fact. Do not layer
on dream haze, neon, liquid objects, or generalized weirdness.

## Selected lead

`01-red-car-impossible-office-lead-4x3.png` is the campaign authority for this
direction. The old red car, wet service landscape, laptop, garment, and data
center remain literal. Only the car interior is impossible: it continues into
a fluorescent glass office far larger than the vehicle can contain.

This image is intentionally preserved exactly as generated. Its garment is a
concept rendering, not a dimensional production proof.

## Supporting studies

1. `02-boardroom-in-creek-study-4x3.png` — a dry black-glass meeting table and
   chrome office chairs stand directly in the moving drainage creek.
2. `03-dry-shirt-on-running-water-study-4x3.png` — evidence/product study in
   which a completely dry garment rests unsupported on the creek surface.
3. `04-rain-product-anchor-study-4x3.png` — direct-flash worn portrait in heavy
   rain. Retained as a tonal and scale anchor; its requested dry-print-field
   impossibility is not sufficiently legible to make it a surreal authority.

## Production boundary

The physical screen-print field is exactly `16in wide × 18in high`, not the
image canvas and not the garment dimensions. Printer-facing SVG and transparent
`4800 × 5400` PNG masters at 300 ppi live in
`../brand-study/source-assets/somewhat-intelligent-shirt/`.

The centered wordmark itself remains the canonical `1.08:1` lockup, placed
without distortion inside the taller 16-by-18-inch field. Generated garment
photographs communicate art direction and approximate placement only.

## Generation provenance

Generated 2026-08-13 using the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The lead
used the existing newer red-car/data-center concept and exact wordmark artwork
as visual references. Shared constraints were exact two-line spelling, a large
front print, wet overcast infrastructure, obsolete consumer machinery, direct
flash, restrained color, and one impossible fact treated as ordinary.
