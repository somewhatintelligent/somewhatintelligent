# FRIEND shirt product copy

## Top-level description

I THINK WE SHOULD BE FRIENDS is printed on a 241 GSM Gildan Hammer 75000: a heavy, boxy shirt with a wide rib collar and dropped shoulder. The front makes the offer. The back explains the access it grants. Printed direct-to-garment on pretreated Pitch Black cotton, because friendship apparently requires documentation.

## Product details

- Gildan Hammer 75000 Maxweight blank
- 241 GSM heavyweight fabric
- Pitch Black: 100% ring-spun cotton
- Boxy, oversized fit
- Rolled-forward drop shoulder
- Wide rib collar
- Side-seamed body
- Taped neck and shoulders
- Direct-to-garment front and back print on a pretreated garment
- Recycled tear-away label

Wash separately before first wear. Wash inside out at 30°C with like colours and tumble dry low. Do not iron the print or dry clean.

## Size and fit comments

Boxy, oversized fit. Measurements are taken from the finished garment laid flat. Measure a shirt you already own and compare chest width and body length.

- Chest width: measured across the garment 1 inch / 2.5 cm below the armhole.
- Body length: measured from the high-point shoulder to the finished hem.

## Production note

The described process is direct-to-garment printing (DTG): the dark cotton garment is pretreated, loaded onto a platen, printed directly by a travelling inkjet printhead, and cured. DTF would print onto transfer film first and then heat-press that film onto the garment.
