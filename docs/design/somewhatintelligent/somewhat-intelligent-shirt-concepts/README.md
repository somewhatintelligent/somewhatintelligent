# SOMEWHAT INTELLIGENT shirt concepts

These are the current campaign and product-image references for the canonical
`SOMEWHAT / INTELLIGENT` garment. They extend the existing FRIEND image world
without turning the wordmark shirt into a second slogan object.

The set alternates image modes rather than repeating one campaign pose:

1. `01-freight-elevator-incident-4x3.png` — direct-flash institutional
   incident; one anonymous adult in the black wordmark shirt inside a scratched
   freight elevator.
2. `02-after-hours-apparatus-work-4x3.png` — technical-office contact image;
   one anonymous adult reaching across a black-glass table among a terminal,
   tractor-feed paper, spilled coffee, chrome, and one red hardware light.
3. `03-front-flat-lay-office-evidence-4x3.png` — front flat-lay product
   authority on stainless steel; full garment silhouette, approved centered
   print, sparse office evidence, and one hot-pink breach.
4. `04-data-center-service-passage-detail-4x3.png` — close worn/detail frame at
   a wet data-center service passage; cotton, ink, collar, and centered chest
   placement remain legible at small display sizes.

The exact outlined artwork in
`../brand-study/source-assets/somewhat-intelligent-shirt/` remains the production
authority. These generated photographs are art-direction and product-display
references; they are not print masters or proof of a specific blank, fit, ink,
or final manufactured scale.

## Generation provenance

Generated 2026-08-13 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. No
CLI/API fallback was used.

Shared references:

- existing FRIEND elevator and after-hours campaign images for direct-flash,
  institutional material, and identity boundaries;
- existing FRIEND product concepts for garment scale, flat-lay evidence, and
  data-center context;
- `somewhat-intelligent-print-white.png` as the authoritative text-artwork
  reference.

Shared constraints:

- exact two-line spelling: `SOMEWHAT` / `INTELLIGENT`;
- centered canonical `1.08:1` lockup with unequal line widths preserved;
- heavyweight black cotton and matte white screen-print behavior;
- near-black, cold steel, oxide gray, and paper white, with one small hot-pink
  breach or red apparatus light where materially justified;
- no founder likeness, extra slogans, logos, tactical imagery, weapons,
  cyberpunk fog, fake HUDs, random crosshairs, gradients, glossy ecommerce
  studio, or watermark.

### `01-freight-elevator-incident-4x3.png`

- Prompt core: one anonymous adult, boxy black wordmark shirt, brushed-steel
  freight elevator, mirror, cold fluorescent ambient, hard direct flash.
- Correction boundary: the mirror carries only the unprinted side/back
  silhouette; it does not invent a second garment graphic.

### `02-after-hours-apparatus-work-4x3.png`

- Prompt core: anonymous adult reaching over a black-glass table, terminal
  laptop, tractor-feed paper, tipped espresso, chrome chair, one red hardware
  status LED.
- Signature: ordinary human work interrupts an otherwise sterile apparatus.

### `03-front-flat-lay-office-evidence-4x3.png`

- Prompt core: complete black T-shirt viewed directly overhead on scratched
  stainless steel, old beige computer edge, black coffee, code-like paper, one
  hot-pink asterisk tab.
- Purpose: the cleanest concept reference for garment silhouette and front
  print placement.

### `04-data-center-service-passage-detail-4x3.png`

- Prompt core: collarbone-to-hips worn crop, open black work jacket, wet ribbed
  data-center service passage at blue-gray dawn, one distant red service light.
- Purpose: test the canonical mark against real cotton, body curvature, outerwear,
  weather, and small display sizes.
